HOW PITCH DETECTION WORKS HERE

The mic's PCM buffer is correlated against itself at every lag; the first strong peak after the direct signal is the note's period, and parabolic interpolation sharpens it to sub-sample precision. ~60 detections/second, all in this tab.

WHAT THE GRADES MEASURE

  • duration — breath support over the hold
  • drift — median cents from the note (embouchure consistency)
  • σ stability — how flat the pitch line is (air control)
